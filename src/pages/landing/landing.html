Questions

Good questions, plain answers.

A few things to know before you begin.

Do I need an account to start?

No. You can practise straight away. Your notes, progress and schedule stay on this device. Online feedback and translation depend on the connected bot service.

Where is my data kept?

Notes, quizzes and your schedule are stored in this browser. Download a backup from Profile. Handwriting images and translation text are sent to the bot service only when you request those online tools. Discord sign-in and cross-device sync are not connected yet.

Which HSK levels are covered?

The app includes starter vocabulary and short reading exercises. Open Study and Reading to see the materials currently available.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The app is built for phones first. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen experience once the install option is available.
